# Week 3 — Raspberry Pi Implementation and 3D Design Testing

## Goal This Week

Complete the **Raspberry Pi implementation for the computer vision system** and make further progress on the **3D design of the drone components**, including initial physical testing of the designed parts.

---

## What We Did

### Raspberry Pi Implementation — Agnivesh

- **Agnivesh** completed the implementation of the **Raspberry Pi system** required for the computer vision project.
- The required software and computer vision components were configured on the Raspberry Pi.
- The system was prepared for further testing and integration with the drone-control system.
- The completed implementation provides the foundation for running the computer vision system in real-time during later stages of the project.

### 3D Design — Amrutha

- **Amrutha** continued working on the **3D design of the drone components**.
- The majority of the design work was completed, with the remaining work focused on final adjustments and improvements.
- The designs were reviewed with respect to the drone's structural requirements, dimensions, mounting, and assembly.
- The components were prepared for physical testing through 3D printing.

### Strength Testing — Team

- As part of testing the **strength and structural suitability** of the 3D-printed components, a **drone leg was printed**.
- The printed leg was used as an initial physical prototype to evaluate the strength and practicality of the design.
- The results of this testing will help determine whether further modifications are required before printing the final components.

---

## Initial Progress

During Week 3, significant progress was made in both the **software and mechanical development** of the project.

**Agnivesh completed the Raspberry Pi implementation**, establishing the software foundation required for the computer vision system. At the same time, **Amrutha almost completed the 3D design work** for the drone components.

A **3D-printed drone leg** was also produced as part of the initial strength testing. This provided an opportunity to evaluate the physical design before proceeding with the final component production.

- Further testing is required to verify the reliability of the Raspberry Pi implementation under actual operating conditions.
- The remaining 3D design work needs to be finalized before producing all the required components.
- Additional strength testing may be required to determine whether the printed components can withstand the expected loads and vibrations of the drone.
- Design modifications may be necessary based on the results of the physical testing.

## Decisions
