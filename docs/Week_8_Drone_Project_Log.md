# Week 8 — Hardware Assembly and PDB Replacement

## Goal This Week

Complete the **hardware assembly of the drone** by successfully implementing the Arduino UNO Q, fixing all components through soldering, and resolving the Power Distribution Board (PDB) failure.

---

## What We Did

### UNO Q Implementation and Hardware Assembly

- The **Arduino UNO Q was successfully implemented** and integrated into the drone system.
- All the required electronic components were positioned and **fixed in place through soldering**.
- The connections between the different components were completed and checked.
- The team progressed toward completing the overall hardware assembly of the drone.

### Power Distribution Board Issue

- During the power-up test, an issue occurred with the **Power Distribution Board (PDB)**.
- The PDB did not have the required **capacitor**, which resulted in an excessive and rapid increase in charge when power was supplied to the board.
- As a result, the **PDB burned out** during the power-up process.
- The team identified the PDB failure and decided to replace the damaged board.

### PDB Replacement and Repair

- A **new PDB was purchased** to replace the damaged one.
- The new PDB was installed during the same week.
- A suitable **capacitor was added to the PDB** to help provide proper power regulation and reduce sudden voltage fluctuations.
- The connections were completed and the board was integrated into the drone's power system.

---

## Initial Progress

During Week 8, significant progress was made toward completing the drone's hardware.

The **Arduino UNO Q was successfully implemented**, and all major electronic components were fixed in position through soldering. However, during the power-up test, the absence of a capacitor on the PDB caused the board to fail and burn out.

The team responded by purchasing a **new PDB**, adding the required capacitor, and installing it within the same week. After completing the necessary connections, the **hardware assembly of the drone was completed**.
