# Week 9 — Pixhawk Calibration and First Flight

## Goal This Week

Configure and calibrate the **Pixhawk flight controller** and complete the necessary ESC connections and firmware setup to prepare the drone for its first flight.

---

## What We Did

### Pixhawk Calibration — QGroundControl

- The team mainly worked on **Pixhawk calibration using QGroundControl (QGC)**.
- The **PX4 firmware was flashed** onto the Pixhawk through QGroundControl.
- Several issues were encountered during the calibration and configuration process.
- The team systematically checked the connections and configuration to identify and resolve the problems.

### ESC Connection Correction

- During the setup, it was found that the **ESC connections were incorrect**.
- The team identified the incorrect connections and corrected them according to the required motor and ESC configuration.
- The corrected connections were then checked before continuing with the calibration process.

### ArduPilot and Mission Planner

- The team also attempted the calibration and configuration process using **ArduPilot firmware with Mission Planner**.
- The required firmware was flashed and the calibration process was carried out.
- However, the **calibration failed in Mission Planner**.
- Due to the unsuccessful calibration, the team returned to **QGroundControl** for further troubleshooting.

### Final PX4 Configuration and First Flight

- The **PX4 firmware was reflashed** through QGroundControl.
- The Pixhawk was recalibrated and the configuration was checked again.
- After resolving the connection and calibration-related issues, the drone was finally prepared for flight.
- The drone successfully completed its **first flight**, marking a major milestone in the project.
