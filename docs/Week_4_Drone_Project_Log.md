## 3D Printing Issue

During Week 4, an issue occurred while producing the 3D-printed components.

- The **PLA filament became out of stock**, so an **AVS filament** was used as an alternative.
- The alternative filament did not work properly with the 3D printer and caused printing problems.
- The filament caused the printer to perform poorly and created extrusion and printing issues.
- As a result, the team was **compelled to manually clean the 3D printer** to remove the accumulated material and restore the printer to a usable condition.
- This issue resulted in delays in the 3D printing process.

## Problems and Blockers

- **PLA filament was out of stock**, affecting the planned 3D printing process.
- The alternative **AVS filament caused printing problems** and negatively affected the printer's operation.
- Manual cleaning of the printer was required to remove the problematic filament and restore proper operation.
- The printing issue caused delays in producing the required drone components.
- Further testing is required to ensure that the refined designs can be printed successfully using suitable filament.

---

## Decisions

- Continue refining the complete 3D design of the drone components.
- Use suitable filament compatible with the 3D printer for future component production.
- Avoid using materials that may cause printer blockage or other mechanical problems unless their compatibility has been verified.
- Continue studying the physics of drone flight, particularly **thrust and lift**, to support the final drone design.
- Perform further physical testing of the printed components before final assembly.
