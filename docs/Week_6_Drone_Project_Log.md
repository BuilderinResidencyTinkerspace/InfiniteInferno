# Week 6 — Frame Selection and UNO Q Implementation

## Goal This Week

Continue the drone's mechanical development by addressing material and hardware availability issues, while simultaneously preparing the implementation on the **Arduino UNO Q**.

---

## What We Did

### 3D Printing and Frame Development

- The team planned to use **carbon-fibre-infused PETG** for printing the drone components because of its higher strength and durability.
- Due to the **unavailability of carbon-fibre-infused PETG**, the team had to use **PLA filament** as an alternative.
- However, PLA does not provide the **required guaranteed strength** for the intended drone application.
- The **unavailability of the required screws and fasteners** also created difficulties in assembling the newly printed frame.
- To overcome these limitations and avoid further delays, the team decided to use a **strong frame that was already available**.
- The available frame provided a more practical solution for continuing the project and moving forward with the remaining development and testing.

### Arduino UNO Q Implementation — Agnivesh

- In parallel with the mechanical work, **Agnivesh** worked on preparing the **implementation on the Arduino UNO Q**.
- The required hardware and software aspects for the UNO Q implementation were studied and prepared.
- This work was carried out simultaneously so that the software and electronics development could progress while the mechanical challenges were being addressed.

---

## Initial Progress

During Week 6, the team faced challenges related to the **availability of suitable 3D-printing material and screws**. Although PLA was available, it could not provide the required guaranteed strength for the final application.

## Problems and Blockers

- **Carbon-fibre-infused PETG was unavailable**, preventing the planned high-strength 3D printing.
- **PLA was used as an alternative**, but its strength could not be guaranteed to meet the project's requirements.
- The required **screws and fasteners were unavailable**, creating difficulties in assembling the newly printed frame.
- These material and hardware limitations affected the planned frame development.

---

## Decisions

- Use PLA only where appropriate while considering its limitations in strength.
- Use the **strong frame already available** to avoid further delays in the project.
- Continue developing and testing the drone using the available frame.
- Continue the **Arduino UNO Q implementation** in parallel with the mechanical development.
- Reconsider the use of a carbon-fibre-infused PETG frame when the required material and hardware become available.
