# Week 2 — Model Refinement and 3D Design

## Goal This Week

Continue the development of the **computer vision model on the Raspberry Pi** while simultaneously working on the **3D design of the drone components** required for the project.

---

## What We Did

### Computer Vision Model — Agnivesh

- **Agnivesh** worked on setting up and deploying the computer vision model on the **Raspberry Pi**.
- The existing model was tested on the Raspberry Pi to evaluate its performance in the project environment.
- The model was further **refined and adjusted** to improve its detection and processing performance.
- Research was carried out on different approaches and techniques that could be used to **improve the accuracy, efficiency, and real-time performance** of the model.

### 3D Design — Amrutha

## Initial Progress

During Week 2, parallel development was carried out in both the **software and mechanical aspects** of the project. The computer vision model was being configured and refined on the Raspberry Pi, while the 3D-printed drone components were being designed.

This helped the team progress simultaneously toward developing the **gesture-recognition system** and the **physical structure of the drone**.

## Problems and Blockers

- Further refinement is required to achieve reliable real-time performance of the computer vision model on the Raspberry Pi.
- Research and testing are still required to determine suitable methods for improving the model's performance.
- The 3D-printed components require careful consideration of dimensions and mounting requirements to ensure proper integration with the drone.
- The software and mechanical components need to be developed with compatibility in mind for later integration.

---

## Decisions

- Continue refining the computer vision model on the Raspberry Pi rather than moving immediately to the drone-control stage.
- Investigate suitable optimization techniques to improve the model's performance.
- Continue developing customized 3D-printed components based on the drone's structural requirements.
- Maintain parallel development of the **computer vision and mechanical design components** to support future system integration.
